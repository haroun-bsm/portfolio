## Audience research (Phase 1 done — 2026-10-29, sources web search)
Key findings from hiring-manager/portfolio content:
- Managers read the **story before the code**: context → challenge → solution → result (README review behavior transfers to case-study pages).
- They evaluate **evidence, not frosting**: problems solved, scope of responsibility, decisions influenced, business outcomes. "Operating evidence" — how the software behaves after the first draft — beats repos alone.
- Depth > breadth: one polished, complete project case study builds more trust than 8 shallow cards.
- The interview question a portfolio must answer: **"What kind of engineer shows up if we hire this person?"**
- Words the audience actually uses: "owned end-to-end", "shipped", "proof of work", "pipeline", "scale (TB/day)", "cost cut by X%", "reliability", "schema", "evaluation", "RAG", "footprint".
- Implication: copy voice = quiet confidence, exact numbers, zero buzzword soup. Design weaponizes typography and whitespace, not gradients.
